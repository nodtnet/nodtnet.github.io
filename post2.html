Amtrak Avelia Liberty Renderings

I submitted a FOIA request back in February for renderings regarding the Amtrak Avelia Liberty train and the FOIA request was fulfilled May 27th 2026.

The request was responded with 13 visible renderings (31 completely censored) submitted by the French manufacturer Alstom to Amtrak before production. The response included rendered images of the interior and exterior of the train created in 2018.
